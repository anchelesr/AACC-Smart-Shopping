# ADR-004 — Shift only non-urgent forecasts to lower-carbon windows

**Status:** Proposed

## The problem

The course requires an observable reaction to a simulated carbon-intensity signal. Forecast refreshes are a reasonable candidate for time shifting; product browsing is not. Waiting indefinitely would, however, undermine the usefulness of the advice.

## Our decision

Evaluate a simulated grid-intensity value and the saved deadline before running each due forecasting job. The proposed policy runs at or below 300 gCO₂e/kWh, may defer above the threshold, and overrides the deferral at the 24-hour deadline. Record the reason for every run or deferral.

## Options we considered

Running every job immediately is simple but ignores the carbon signal. Moving workloads between regions is much more complex and may create latency or data-location problems. Reducing the frequency of price collection is not an adequate substitute, since that can make observations stale without proving lower emissions.

## What this means for the project

The policy introduces scheduling and recovery logic and may produce older forecasts during high-intensity periods. We will compare identical immediate and delayed workloads, including retries and job runtime. Lower emissions are an outcome to test, not something the design guarantees.
