# ADR-004 — Adapt operation to a simulated carbon signal through operating modes

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-25)  
**Revision note:** Rewritten in revision 2; supersedes the time-shift-only decision. Extended in revision 3 with electricity price, a renewable flag, event notification ([ADR-012](ADR-012.md)) and workload placement ([ADR-013](ADR-013.md)).

## The problem

The course requires an observable reaction to a simulated carbon-intensity signal. The first revision only read the signal and deferred forecast jobs, which did not show how the application becomes aware of carbon, how it changes its configuration and how its behaviour follows. Waiting indefinitely would also undermine the usefulness of the advice.

## Our decision

Introduce a sense, decide, act and feedback loop. The Carbon Monitor samples and validates the signals (carbon intensity, electricity price and a renewable-availability flag, from a simulator or optionally Electricity Maps) every 5 minutes. The Policy Engine classifies intensity and price separately against a versioned policy with hysteresis, takes the more restrictive class, lets available renewable energy force GREEN, applies an operator override and persists an operating mode (GREEN, AMBER or RED) with a reason and parameters; a stale signal falls back to GREEN behaviour. All consumers read the mode through one interface (pull, cached up to 60 s) and change their behaviour: forecast jobs, worker concurrency, assistant routing, cache lifetime and optional batches. The persisted 24 h job deadline overrides the mode, and the mandatory daily import is never carbon-shifted. The operator edits the policy at runtime; defaults live in Git. Thresholds are configurable demonstration values.

## Options we considered

- **Defer forecast jobs only (revision 1):** simple, but it shows no awareness beyond one component and no change of configuration.
- **Every component reads the carbon feed itself:** duplicates one concern, multiplies external calls and can give components different views of the same moment.
- **Push events only:** reacts faster but a lost event would leave a consumer in the wrong mode; revision 3 therefore adds events *on top of* the pulled, cached mode, which stays the source of truth ([ADR-012](ADR-012.md)).
- **Move workloads between regions:** much more complex and may create latency and data-location problems; a simulated node-pool placement is used instead ([ADR-013](ADR-013.md)).
- **Reduce price collection frequency in high-carbon periods:** makes observations stale without evidence of lower emissions.

## What this means for the project

There are more states and a policy to test, and older forecasts may appear during long RED periods, limited by the deadline. The mode semantics, thresholds and hysteresis are design values that the evaluation must validate. Lower emissions are an outcome to measure against an immediate-run baseline, not a property of the design.
