# ADR-014 — Observability with Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry and measured energy

**Status:** Proposed (2026-11-25), to be confirmed by the authors

## The problem

Sustainability and quality claims need evidence. The course exercises instrumented an application with Prometheus counters, histograms and gauges, built Grafana dashboards and mentioned Kepler and CodeCarbon for energy; distributed tracing shows where a request spends its time. Monitoring (is something wrong?) and observability (why?) are different needs.

## Our decision

Use Prometheus for metrics (pull from annotated pods, Pushgateway for short-lived Jobs), Grafana for dashboards and alerts, OpenTelemetry for traces with one trace id carried into the assistant trace and the job logs, and structured logs. Metrics have bounded labels. Energy is reported from two sources and labelled by method: an estimate (power x time, token factor) and a measurement (Kepler for pods, CodeCarbon for Python jobs), with the caveat that both can fall back to estimation models in a virtualised cluster. Dashboards are separate for the operator, the engineer, sustainability and AI quality; alerts are few and each names a first action. The metric catalogue is in [Observability](../observability.md).

## Options we considered

- **Application logs only:** cheap but cannot show rates, distributions or energy.
- **A managed monitoring service:** less to run, but costs money, adds lock-in and extra plugins are harder; Prometheus and Grafana are also what the course uses.
- **Only estimates for energy:** works everywhere, but cannot validate the assumptions.
- **A full log stack from the start:** useful later; structured logs with trace ids are enough now.

## What this means for the project

More containers to run and more instrumentation code. Metric names and labels become a contract that must be kept stable, and unbounded labels would overload Prometheus. The energy numbers remain estimates unless the measurement works on our cluster; the report must say which source produced each figure.
